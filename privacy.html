Privacy Policy

RAF Aptitude Test Prep · Effective date: 19 July 2026

Developer: MD Shahariar Nazim Joy · Contact: dmjks120@gmail.com

Summary

RAF Aptitude Test Prep is an offline-first Defence Aptitude Assessment (DAA) style practice app. We do not use third-party analytics SDKs or advertising SDKs. We do not sell personal data.

Data we process

  • On-device study data: practice answers, bookmarks, streaks, study plan preferences, and mock results are stored locally on your device using Apple frameworks (e.g. SwiftData / UserDefaults) on iPhone and iPad, or the app's private storage (SharedPreferences) on Android.
  • Purchases: Subscription and lifetime unlocks are processed by Apple through StoreKit / App Store (iPhone, iPad) or by Google through Google Play Billing (Android). We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Notifications (optional): If you allow notifications, a local daily reminder may be scheduled on your device. Reminder scheduling does not send your data to our servers.

Data we do not collect

We do not operate an account system. We do not upload your contacts, photos, microphone, or health data. The app does not include third-party analytics or ad networks.

Sharing

We do not share personal study data with third parties. Apple or Google may process purchase-related information under Apple’s privacy policy or Google’s privacy policy as part of App Store or Google Play transactions.

Children

This app is intended for adults preparing for recruiting aptitude assessments. It is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

  • Reset study progress in Settings.
  • Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple ID subscription settings, or on Android in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions.
  • Disable notifications in iOS Settings or Android Settings → Apps → RAF Aptitude Prep → Notifications.
  • Delete the app to remove on-device study data.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
